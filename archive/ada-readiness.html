ADA Title II Accessibility Readiness

WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance

The compliance deadline is real. Is your website ready?

The DOJ's ADA Title II final rule (published April 24, 2024) requires state and local government web content and mobile apps to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards. Larger entities face a two-year compliance window. Smaller entities have three years.

This is not optional. It applies to websites, PDFs, forms, videos, third-party tools embedded on your site, and mobile applications. If your organization serves the public digitally, your digital presence must be accessible.

TR3 Media Solutions already builds to WCAG 2.1 AA standards on every project. We can help you understand where you stand today and build a clear path to compliance.

What's at Stake

Non-compliance is a legal, reputational, and operational risk.

  • Legal exposure: DOJ enforcement actions, complaints filed by advocacy organizations, and private lawsuits are increasing across state and local government
  • Constituent exclusion: Inaccessible websites prevent residents with disabilities from accessing public services, information, and programs they are entitled to
  • Procurement risk: Federal and state funding increasingly require digital accessibility documentation as a condition of grants and contracts
  • Reputational harm: Public-facing organizations that fail accessibility standards risk erosion of community trust
How We Help

Accessibility audit, remediation, and governance.

01

Accessibility Readiness Audit

A structured review of your website against WCAG 2.1 AA success criteria. We test across pages, forms, PDFs, embedded tools, and media. You receive a prioritized report with specific issues, severity ratings, and remediation guidance.

02

Remediation Sprint

Hands-on fixes to address critical and high-priority accessibility issues. This includes HTML/CSS corrections, ARIA implementation, color contrast adjustments, image alt text, form labeling, keyboard navigation, and document remediation.

Accessibility Governance Plan

A documented framework your team can follow to maintain compliance over time. Includes content authoring guidelines, testing checklists, vendor requirements, and a review cadence so new content stays compliant after launch.

04

Ongoing Monitoring Partnership

Quarterly accessibility reviews, staff training sessions, and priority support for new content and features. Designed for organizations that publish frequently and need a reliable compliance partner.
