Guarantee Terms

Marketing That Pays — 90-Day Performance Guarantee

The Guarantee

Complete all 6 modules of Marketing That Pays. Implement the system within 90 days of program completion. If you do not see measurable improvement in your marketing pipeline, Domonique Davis and the TR3 Media team will continue working with you at no additional cost until you do.

This is a results commitment, not a refund policy.

Eligibility Requirements

To activate the guarantee, all of the following must be true:

  • Full program completion. You must complete all 6 modules, including all exercises, worksheets, and implementation steps outlined in each module.
  • Documented implementation. You must demonstrate that the revenue system was implemented as designed within 90 calendar days of completing the final module. Implementation includes deploying the offer architecture, installing tracking, activating at least one paid or organic campaign, and building the reporting dashboard.
  • Good faith participation. You must attend all scheduled cohort sessions (or review recordings within 48 hours), participate in feedback loops, and respond to implementation check-ins.
  • Access to data. You must provide TR3 Media with access to relevant analytics, ad accounts, and CRM data necessary to evaluate pipeline performance.

What Counts as "Measurable Improvement"

The guarantee is activated if none of the following outcomes are observed within 90 days of full implementation:

  • An increase in qualified inbound conversations directly attributable to the marketing system
  • Improved attribution clarity (knowing which channels drive revenue)
  • Confirmed reduction in wasted marketing spend
  • A functioning pipeline that converts attention into revenue-generating activity

At least one of these outcomes must be demonstrably absent for the guarantee to apply.

What "Continued Support" Includes

If the guarantee is activated, TR3 Media will provide:

  • Up to 8 additional weeks of implementation support
  • Bi-weekly strategy calls (30 minutes each)
  • Targeted revisions to campaign assets, offer messaging, or tracking configuration
  • Written recommendations for system adjustments

Continued support does not include new project scope, additional ad spend, third-party tool subscriptions, or services outside the original program deliverables.

Exclusions

The guarantee does not apply in the following situations:

  • The participant did not complete all 6 modules
  • The system was not implemented within the 90-day window
  • The participant made material changes to the system without consulting TR3 Media
  • The participant's business model, offer, or market fundamentally changed during the program
  • External factors outside the scope of marketing (e.g., legal issues, partnership dissolution, industry shutdown) made implementation impractical

How to Activate

To activate the guarantee, email hello@tr3mediasolutions.com with the subject line "Guarantee Activation" within 90 days of completing implementation. Include:

  • Confirmation of module completion dates
  • Summary of what was implemented
  • Access to analytics and campaign data
  • A brief description of the outcomes (or lack thereof) observed

TR3 Media will review within 5 business days and respond with a proposed support plan.

Governing Terms

This guarantee is a supplementary commitment and does not modify the terms of the enrollment agreement. In the event of a conflict, the enrollment agreement governs. This guarantee applies only to participants enrolled in the Marketing That Pays program and is non-transferable.
