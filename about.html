The Agency

Strategic design for organizations that carry real responsibility.

TR3 Media Solutions is a creative agency for mission-driven organizations that need clarity, consistency, and systems that actually work.

We partner with teams whose work carries responsibility — public trust, community impact, educational outcomes, civic engagement. When the work matters, the design has to do more than look good. It has to function under pressure, scale with capacity, and communicate with intention.

We don't chase trends. We design with context, constraints, and long-term use in mind.

Meet the Founder

Domonique Davis

TR3 Media Solutions is led by Domonique Davis, a creative director with over a decade of experience across digital design, user experience, campaign development, and public-sector work.

How We Think

Principles that guide our work

  • Good design creates clarity, not confusion. The goal is always to make it easier for your audience to understand who you are and why your work matters.
  • Structure protects creativity. Process isn't the enemy of inspiration — it's what gives creative work room to succeed under real constraints.
  • The best partnerships are built on mutual respect for process. We bring creative leadership. You bring organizational knowledge. The work is better because of both.

We don't make things pretty. We make them clear.

Most agencies optimize for aesthetics. We optimize for clarity, durability, and trust. The organizations we serve can't afford marketing that looks good but doesn't hold up under scrutiny. Our job is to build systems that work — for your team, your stakeholders, and the communities you serve.
