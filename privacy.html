Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through our website, we collect the information you provide, including your name, organization name, role, email address, and project details. We do not collect information automatically through cookies or tracking scripts unless specifically disclosed.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to evaluate project inquiries, communicate with you about potential engagements, and manage our client relationships. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain inquiry data for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected. If an engagement does not proceed, inquiry data is typically archived or deleted within 12 months.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, or form processing. These services may collect technical data such as IP addresses and browser information. We select service providers that maintain reasonable privacy and security practices.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at hello@tr3mediasolutions.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact
