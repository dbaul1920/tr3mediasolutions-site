The Program

6 modules. 4 weeks. One complete brand system built by you.

01

The Audit

Where are you starting from?

Before we build anything, we inventory everything. What exists, what's missing, what's inconsistent, what's costing you time. You can't build a solid system on top of chaos — so we clear the slate and get honest about where you are.

02

The Foundation

Who you are. Who you serve.

Mission, core values, and the three audience profiles every nonprofit actually operates within — the people you serve, your funders, and your network partners. Strategy before visuals. This layer is where most organizations are working from a shaky base without knowing it.

03

See It First

Developing your eye. The CRAP Framework.

Contrast. Repetition. Alignment. Proximity. Plus typography. Most programs skip this entirely. We teach you to recognize what's off before you try to fix it — so you can articulate the standard, not just feel it. This is how you go from "I'll know it when I see it" to "I can tell you exactly what's wrong and why."

Signature Module
04

Build Your Brand

Get it out of your head.

Logo files, color palette, typography, photography direction, tone of voice. Every visual and verbal element documented so anyone who touches your brand knows exactly what to do — without tracking you down to ask.

05

Standards & Access

Who are you leaving out?

Language requirements, mobile optimization, screen reader compatibility, ADA considerations. The standards that make your brand work for everyone in the community you serve — and hold up to the expectations of the funders and partners watching.

06

Install & Activate

Plug it in. Turn it on.

Package everything into your brand system and connect it to every tool you already use. Test it. Share it. From this point forward, your brand travels with you — into every platform, every AI tool, every new hire, every future project. This is the flywheel. This is what compounds.
