Messaging Guidelines

Do

  • Lead with clarity, follow with craft
  • Speak to the mission, not the aesthetics
  • Acknowledge the difficulty of the client's work
  • Use concrete language: "stakeholders," "timelines," "scrutiny"
  • Emphasize durability and maintainability

Don't

  • Call things "beautiful" or "stunning" — say clear, effective, durable
  • Use buzzwords like "synergy," "disrupt," or "cutting-edge"
  • Position design as decoration; it's a structural tool
  • Overpromise — the tone is honest and grounded
  • Be casual to the point of being flippant
