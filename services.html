How We Support Your Work

Our Services

Strategic creative support across digital, print, and campaign environments.

Most clients engage TR3 Media through an ongoing creative partnership. Project engagements are available when scope is clear, timelines are real, and the work benefits from a strategic partner.

Engagements are shaped by scope, complexity, and the level of partnership required. Every project begins with strategic alignment — because design without direction is just decoration.

01

Ongoing Creative Partnership

For teams that need consistent, senior-level design support without rebuilding context every week.

This is our primary engagement model. We retain institutional knowledge, protect standards, and keep execution moving with clear priorities and clean systems. You get a creative director who understands your organization — not a rotating roster of freelancers.

  • Dedicated creative leadership embedded in your workflow
  • Brand consistency across all touchpoints and stakeholders
  • Reduced revision cycles through institutional knowledge
02

Brand Architecture & Messaging

For organizations that need alignment, clear decisions, and a brand system people can actually follow.

Includes brand clarification, visual standards, campaign direction, and creative leadership that keeps work consistent across people, platforms, and vendors. We clarify your positioning, voice, and narrative so stakeholders understand your impact and direction.

  • Stronger donor confidence and clearer board communication
  • Visual and messaging standards your team can maintain
  • Positioning that reflects the scale of your mission
03

Digital Experience Strategy

For teams that need a site built for clarity, accessibility, and long-term maintainability.

We focus on structure, content flow, and governance so the site stays useful after launch. Ideal for fundraising, public engagement, and program visibility. All sites meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.

  • Websites designed for fundraising and public trust
  • Content architecture your team can manage independently
  • Accessibility-first design meeting WCAG 2.1 AA standards
04

Campaign & Communications Leadership

For launches and initiatives that need cohesion across channels and stakeholders.

From gala season to media moments, we design cohesive campaign systems that stay aligned across channels, timelines, and stakeholders. Includes messaging frameworks, campaign visuals, presentation design, and multi-channel assets built to work together — not compete for attention.

  • Unified messaging across print, digital, and social
  • Campaign systems that reduce last-minute chaos
  • Presentation decks and stakeholder materials
05

Print & Event Design

For reports, collateral, and events that need to look as strong in person as they do on screen.

From layout to production support, we design with real-world constraints in mind and keep systems consistent across formats. Print and event design that matches your digital standards and works in production reality.

  • Event branding from invitations to environmental signage
  • Annual reports and donor collateral
  • Production-ready files with vendor coordination
