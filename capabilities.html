Company Overview

TR3 Media Solutions is a strategic creative partner for foundations, school districts, civic organizations, and mission-driven teams. We help public-facing organizations align brand, messaging, and digital execution so their work is clear, accessible, and built to last. Based in Detroit, Michigan, TR3 has over a decade of experience supporting civic and nonprofit initiatives, with a particular focus on stakeholder-heavy environments where design must function under scrutiny, scale with capacity, and communicate with intention.

Core Competencies

Brand Architecture & Messaging Positioning, voice, narrative systems, and visual standards for organizations with complex stakeholder landscapes.
Digital Experience Strategy Websites and digital platforms built for clarity, accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA), and long-term maintainability.
Campaign & Communications Cohesive campaign systems across print, digital, and experiential channels for launches, galas, and initiatives.
Accessibility & Compliance ADA Title II readiness, WCAG 2.1 AA audits, remediation, and ongoing governance for state and local entities.
Print & Event Design Annual reports, donor collateral, event branding, signage, and production-ready files with vendor coordination.
